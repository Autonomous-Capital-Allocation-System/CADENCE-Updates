# CADENCE Changelog

Private alpha series. Installers are attached to each pre-release on the
CADENCE releases page (collaborator access during the alpha). Quit CADENCE
fully before installing over an earlier version; your workspace, settings,
and approvals are kept.

## 0.1.0-alpha.15
- Buffer rail: the per-network details Buffer requires (Facebook and Instagram
  post type, Google Business type, YouTube title) are sent for you; without
  them Buffer refused every Facebook and Instagram post.
- A post the calendar flags as needing attention is never handed to Buffer,
  even in draft mode.
- Verified live against a real Buffer account.

## 0.1.0-alpha.14
- Buffer rail: connect your own Buffer account, map channels, hand approved
  posts over as drafts or scheduled posts; Send to Buffer, Send Approved Now,
  automatic hand-off in Autonomous mode, Confirm Sent Posts.
- Check for Updates reads this feed; support, privacy, terms, and security
  pages on acasintelligence.com are linked from the app.

## 0.1.0-alpha.13
- Redraft in Review the Week no longer crashes the app.
- Approval Queue: Select All, Approve Selected, Reject Selected, Approve Newest
  Pipeline Revision, Approve All Shown; readable rows with each draft's origin.
- Dialogs that are drafting refuse to close until the draft finishes; quitting
  waits for background checks.

## 0.1.0-alpha.12
- Review the Week: read every planned post due in the next seven days and
  approve the ones that are right; Approve All Remaining unlocks only after
  every draft has been opened; changed files are refused until adopted or
  redrafted.

## 0.1.0-alpha.11
- Posting Plans: a standing rhythm of slots drafted ahead into calendar pieces
  with their own approvals; nags at 72, 24, 6, and 1 hours and once when
  missed; Telegram /plan and relayed nags.

## 0.1.0-alpha.10
- Illustrated user guide ships with each release; Settings opens at its full
  height; builds published as pre-releases with installers, guide, docs, and
  checksums.

## 0.1.0-alpha.9
- Plain ACAS-family update manifest with a browser handoff; Chrome Helper
  pairing fixed; optional signing steps in the build; beta-readiness
  checklist.

## 0.1.0-alpha.8
- Performance & Learning: source-attributed signal imports, observations, and
  separate advisory suggestions.

## 0.1.0-alpha.7
- Glass interface; durable events, local events API, signed webhooks.

## 0.1.0-alpha.6
- Setlist handoff v2 with a stable campaign key and a fail-closed outcomes
  import.

## 0.1.0-alpha.5
- Release-sized creative variants behind the review loop, with provenance.

## 0.1.0-alpha.3 and alpha.4
- Release Details, the Content Calendar with four publishing states, Telegram
  calendar view, and the first round of native Windows fixes.

## 0.1.0-alpha.2
- First cross-platform test builds.
